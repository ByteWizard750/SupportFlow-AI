# Account Access and Security FAQ

## Password Reset Procedure
If you are unable to remember your password or encounter login authentication errors:
1. Navigate to the login screen and click 'Forgot Password'.
2. Enter your registered account email address.
3. Check your inbox for a secure password reset link (valid for 60 minutes).
4. Follow the link to enter and confirm your new password. Passwords must be at least 10 characters and contain letters, numbers, and symbols.

## Session Expiration and Inactivity Timeouts
For enterprise security compliance, active user sessions automatically expire after 30 minutes of continuous inactivity. If your session expires while editing, simply refresh the login screen and re-authenticate.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
SupportFlow AI supports Time-based One-Time Password (TOTP) 2FA using apps such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password:
- To enable 2FA: Go to Account Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication.
- If you lose access to your 2FA device, use the emergency backup recovery codes generated during initial setup.
- If recovery codes are also unavailable, an organization Owner must contact Support to initiate identity verification.

## Account Lockout Policy
To prevent brute-force attacks, accounts are automatically locked for 15 minutes after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. You can wait 15 minutes for the lockout to expire or trigger a password reset to unlock your account immediately.

## User Roles and Access Permissions
Four default permission tiers are available:
- Owner: Full administrative and billing authority.
- Admin: Manages users, integrations, and operational settings.
- Member: Creates and handles tickets, views reports.
- Viewer: Read-only access to ticket queues and dashboards.
Role assignments can be adjusted by Owners or Admins under Account Settings > Team Members.
