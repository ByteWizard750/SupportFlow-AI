# Billing and Payment Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods
SupportFlow AI accepts the following payment methods:
- Credit and Debit Cards: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover.
- Digital Wallets: PayPal.
- Invoicing and Wire Transfer / ACH: Available exclusively for Enterprise annual contracts with order minimums of $5,000.

## Billing Cycles
Subscriptions are billed in advance on either a monthly or annual cadence:
- Monthly Billing: Charged automatically on the same calendar day each month matching the subscription start date.
- Annual Billing: Charged once per year and includes a standard 15% discount compared to monthly pricing.

## Updating Payment Details
Billing administrators can update stored credit cards or payment details at any time by navigating to:
Account Settings > Billing & Invoices > Payment Methods.
Updated cards take effect immediately for all subsequent billing cycles.

## Failed Payments and Grace Period
If an automatic payment attempt fails:
1. An automated notification email is sent to the account owner and billing administrator.
2. The payment processing system will automatically re-attempt the charge on Day 1, Day 3, and Day 7 following the initial failure.
3. The account enters a 7-day grace period where all services remain fully operational.
4. If payment is not resolved by the end of Day 7, the account is temporarily suspended until valid payment details are provided.

## Invoices and Receipts
PDF invoices and receipts with itemized tax details are automatically generated for every transaction and can be downloaded from the Billing & Invoices portal.
